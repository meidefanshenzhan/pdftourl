Good to know

Which seller videos can I use?+

Product demos, installation tutorials, feature introductions and after-sales instructions, as well as other spoken videos. Review brands, model numbers, specifications and translations before publishing.

Will my video get a public download link?+

Audio is processed online to generate subtitles. Video rendering runs locally in your browser. No public file link is created; download your results before refreshing.

How is the free allowance counted?+

Transcription and one selected translation count as one generation. Visitors get 3 successful transcriptions daily; an access code unlocks access for one hour. Shared daily limits still apply. Contact us if you need more generations.

Can I preview subtitles on the video?+

Play your video here with the selected subtitle track. After rendering, preview and download an MP4 with subtitles burned into the picture.
