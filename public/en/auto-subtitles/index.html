Good to know

Which seller videos can I use?+

Product demos, installation tutorials, feature introductions and after-sales instructions, as well as other spoken videos. Review brands, model numbers, specifications and translations before publishing.

Will my video get a public download link?+

Your browser extracts audio and sends it to Cloudflare AI when you generate subtitles. No public file link is created. Subtitles stay in this page, so export them before refreshing.

How is the free allowance counted?+

Transcription and one selected translation count as one generation. Processing pauses when the shared daily capacity is exhausted; try later or contact us.

Can I preview subtitles on the video?+

Play your video here with the selected subtitle track. Downloaded SRT / VTT files are separate subtitle files that you can import into a video editor.
