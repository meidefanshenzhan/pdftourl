How to combine keyword lists

Enter one word or complete phrase per line. Choose order, separator and affixes, review the estimate, then generate. Each combination takes one entry from every active list, preserving each list’s entry order. Affixes are added exactly as entered; include any spaces you need.

GOOD TO KNOW

Good to know

Can an entry contain several words?+

Yes. A phrase such as waterproof backpack stays together as one entry in the combinations.

How is the number of combinations calculated?+

Multiply the sizes of the two lists, then multiply by the third list if enabled. For example, 10 × 20 × 5 gives 1,000 candidates. With deduplication on, lists are deduplicated first; identical joined results can reduce the final count.

How do I change word order?+

Choose forward or reverse order: A+B or B+A for two lists, and A+B+C or C+B+A for three lists.

How can I export the result?+

TXT puts each combination on a separate line. CSV exports every result under a Keyword column. Cells starting with spreadsheet formula symbols receive a safety prefix for viewing in spreadsheets.

Where are my lists saved?+

Combinations are processed in your browser. Inputs are not sent to a server or automatically saved as drafts. Download your results if you want to keep them.
