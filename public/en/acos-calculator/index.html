GOOD TO KNOW

Good to know

How are ACOS and ROAS related?+

ACOS is ad spend divided by ad-attributed revenue, expressed as a percentage. ROAS is ad-attributed revenue divided by ad spend. An ACOS of 20% corresponds to a ROAS of 5.

Can I enter commission as an amount?+

Yes. Choose a percentage of price or a fixed amount, using your actual applicable fee. Allocate other costs per item.

What happens at zero CVR or zero CPC?+

At zero CVR, there are no modeled converted orders, so per-order ad cost and profit show “—”. At zero CPC with positive CVR, modeled ad cost is zero and ROAS shows “—” because ad spend is its denominator.

How does target profit affect the CPC limit?+

A higher target leaves less contribution for ads. A target equal to contribution before ads leaves a zero CPC limit; a higher target is flagged as unattainable under the current inputs.

Are inputs saved?+

Calculations run in your browser without uploading or automatically saving inputs. Copy results or download CSV / TXT to keep your assumptions and outputs.
