Good to know

What does list coverage mean?+

It is the number of matched keywords divided by the number of unique target keywords in the selected scope. It checks your copy and does not measure search ranking or marketplace indexing.

Can a phrase be split across different fields?+

No. An exact phrase must appear together in a single field. All-words mode also requires every word within one field; it does not combine title and bullet text to create a match.

How are symbols and non-English text handled?+

Exact phrase mode matches text and symbols literally. Whole-word boundaries use letters, combining marks, numbers and underscores. Turn whole words off to check a substring within continuous Chinese text.

Is my copy uploaded or saved?+

Checks and exports run in your browser. Your input is not sent to the server and drafts are not saved automatically. Save anything you need before refreshing or closing the page.
