Good to know

Are my images uploaded?+

Image processing and ZIP creation happen in your browser. Image content is not sent to the server and files are not saved automatically. Select your files again after refreshing or closing the page.

Does lower quality always mean a smaller file?+

Lower JPG and WebP quality usually reduces size, but results vary. PNG ignores the quality slider; resizing can reduce its pixel count. Check the preview and actual output size.

What happens to transparency and orientation?+

PNG / WebP output retains transparency; JPG uses a white background. Your browser decodes orientation information before re-encoding. Check the resulting orientation and colors.

How can I keep images sharp?+

Start with 82% quality and a 2000px longest edge, then adjust for your task. For text, fine lines or detailed artwork, raise quality or choose PNG. Check marketplace format and dimension requirements before uploading.
