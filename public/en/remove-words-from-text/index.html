Good to know

Is my text uploaded or saved?+

Filtering runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to the server and drafts are not saved automatically.

Can I remove phrases and special characters?+

Yes. Enter one word or phrase per line. Terms such as C++ and red shoes match literally, not as regular expressions.

How does Match all terms work?+

Every term must appear on the same line, in any order. Word order and spacing inside each phrase must still match.

Can I use the output as negative keywords?+

Use it to prepare a candidate list. Review conversions, spending and advertising match types before applying it to a campaign.
