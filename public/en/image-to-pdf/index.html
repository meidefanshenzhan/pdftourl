Good to know

Are my images uploaded?+

Image selection, ordering and PDF creation happen in your browser. Image content is not sent to the server and files are not saved automatically. Select files again after refreshing or closing the page.

What is the difference between contain and cover?+

Contain fits the full image inside the margins and may leave blank space. Cover fills the area inside the margins and crops image content that extends beyond it.

How can I change page order?+

The image list becomes the PDF page order. Move images up or down, or sort filenames naturally, so image2 comes before image10.

What is the resulting PDF like?+

Pages are made from images, preserving the appearance of text within them. Images are re-encoded with transparency filled white. Sharpness and file size depend on your originals and quality settings; check the preview.
